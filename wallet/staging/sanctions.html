Sanctioned Jurisdictions and Restricted Persons (DRAFT)

You may not participate in, sign up for, or receive anything from this program if you are: (a) located in, ordinarily resident in, or organized under the laws of any country or region subject to comprehensive U.S. sanctions; (b) listed on, or owned 50% or more or controlled by a person listed on, the U.S. Treasury OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) List or any other U.S. sanctions list; or (c) acting on behalf of any such person. Check the SDN List at https://sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov/ and the sanctions programs at https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-programs-and-country-information. We may screen wallet addresses and participants, block access by geography, and withhold or revoke points or anything else from anyone at our discretion to comply with law. You may not use a VPN or other means to evade these restrictions.
